Why book through me?

Because I'm an independent musician and not part of a larger corporation or booking agency, I'm able to operate with fewer middlemen and lower overhead expenses - a win-win for clients who can pay fewer fees and musicians who can still be paid competitively.

Whatever style of music you're looking for, you'll get a band that's 100% live and real - no prerecorded tracks, no lip-syncing. I'm a believer in live music truly being live music!
